String Shot — Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026 Applies to the String Shot Android app Developer Mazen Afaneh

1. Overview

String Shot is a pull-the-string hockey and skee-ball style game. You can play against the computer, a friend on one device, or a friend on another phone using a short room code. This policy explains what data the app handles and who it passes through. There is no account system, no sign-in, no email collection, and no access to your contacts, photos, files, or precise location.

2. Information we collect

Stored only on your device

Your settings are saved locally on your phone so they are remembered next time: the optional player nicknames you type in (up to 8 characters), your chosen game speed, and sound on/off. This information stays on your device unless you play online (see below). Removing the app deletes it.

Online play

When you create or join an online room, the app connects to a small relay server we run on Cloudflare. It passes game messages between you and your opponent: the 4-letter room code, your nickname, the game mode, and your shots and turns. The server does not run the game, does not store these messages, and discards everything when the room closes. Your opponent sees the nickname you chose, so avoid using anything that identifies you. As with any internet connection, Cloudflare receives your IP address in order to carry the connection.

Advertising data

String Shot shows an occasional full-screen ad between matches. Ads are provided by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and its ad network partners, including Unity Ads. To serve and measure ads, these services collect or access device and advertising identifiers (such as your Android advertising ID), approximate (non-precise) location derived from your IP address, information about how you interact with ads and the app, device and diagnostic information, and a list of installed apps (used for ad relevance and fraud prevention). This happens through the ad companies' SDKs, not code we write.

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, precise location, photos, or contacts. The app never asks for them.

3. Third-party services

  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and Unity Ads — serve the ads described above. Their handling of advertising data is governed by Unity's policies: Unity Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare — hosts the online-play relay server. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play — distributes the app and may provide us with anonymous install and crash statistics.

4. How we use information

Data stored on your device is used only to run the game. Online-play messages are used only to connect you with your opponent during a match. Advertising data is used by the ad companies and their partners to select, deliver, and measure ads and to prevent fraud. We do not sell your data and do not use it for anything beyond running the game and showing ads.

5. Your choices

  • Nicknames are optional. You can leave the defaults or use any name you like. Avoid using anything that identifies you, especially for online play.
  • Limit ad personalization. On Android, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads to delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads.
  • Play offline. Playing against the computer or a friend on one device does not use online play.
  • Delete local data. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove everything saved on your device.

6. Data retention and security

We do not keep any personal data about you. Online-play messages are not stored and are discarded when the room closes. Data on your device stays there until you clear it or uninstall the app. Data handled by the ad companies is retained under their own policies.

7. Children's privacy

String Shot is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us below.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
