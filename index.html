String Shot — Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026 Applies to the String Shot Android app Developer Mazen Afaneh

1. Overview

String Shot is a pull-the-string hockey and skee-ball style game you play against the computer or a friend on one device. This policy explains what little data the app handles and who it passes through. There is no account system, no sign-in, no email collection, and no access to your contacts, photos, files, or precise location.

2. Information we collect

Stored only on your device

Your settings are saved locally on your phone so they are remembered next time: the optional player nicknames you type in (up to 8 characters), your chosen game speed, and sound on/off. This information stays on your device. It is not sent to us or to any server, and removing the app deletes it.

Advertising data

String Shot may show ads through Google AdMob, such as an optional rewarded ad to retry a shot or an occasional full-screen ad between matches. AdMob and its ad partners may collect device and advertising identifiers, approximate (non-precise) location, and app usage data to serve and measure ads. This happens through Google's SDK, not code we write.

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, precise location, photos, or contacts. The app never asks for them. The nicknames you enter are never uploaded and are not shown to anyone else.

3. Third-party services

  • Google AdMob — serves the ads described above. Google's policies govern its handling of advertising data: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob data disclosure.
  • Google Play — distributes the app and may provide us with anonymous install and crash statistics.

4. How we use information

Data stored on your device is used only to run the game. Advertising data is used by Google and its partners to select and measure ads. We do not sell your data and do not use it for anything beyond running the game and showing ads.

5. Your choices

  • Nicknames are optional. You can leave the defaults ("You" and "Friend") or use any name you like. Avoid using anything that identifies you.
  • Limit ad personalization. On Android, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads to reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads.
  • Delete local data. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove everything saved on your device.

6. Data retention and security

We do not run a server for String Shot and do not keep any personal data about you. Data on your device stays there until you clear it or uninstall the app. Data handled by Google's ad services is retained under Google's own policies.

7. Children's privacy

String Shot is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us below.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
